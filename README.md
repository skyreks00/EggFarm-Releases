## What it does

- **Never stops farming.** Relaunches Roblox when it crashes, freezes or gets disconnected, and restarts your executor if it closes.
- **Live dashboard** on your PC and your phone: money per second, speed, players, your hub's current activity, events.
- **Your eggs.** Growing eggs with the real time left, and a **Hatch** button as soon as one is ready.
- **Your inventory.** Every pet and every egg in your bag, with search, filters and sorting. **Place** an egg on your base or **Sell** a pet or an egg from anywhere.
- **Phone access** through a secure link: scan a QR code once, then open it from anywhere. No account, no port to open.
- **Optional Chili Hub** loading, so the hub starts with the game.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and Roblox.
- An **executor** with an `autoexec` folder and a `workspace` folder, with **auto-attach** (auto-inject) and **auto-execute** turned on.
- A **key** from the person who gave you Egg Farm.

## Install

1. **Download** `EggFarm.exe` and put it in its own folder, for example `Documents\EggFarm`.
2. **Run it.** Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* because the program isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. **Activate.** A page opens in your browser: paste your key (it starts with `EF1-`) and click **Activate**. A key works on one PC only: the first one that activates it.
4. **Set it up.** The settings page walks you through it:
   - **Your executor:** pick it in the list (open it first so Egg Farm finds it), or add it by hand.
   - **In-game scripts:** click **Install scripts**, then make sure auto-execute is on in your executor.
   - **Options:** auto-restart Roblox, start with Windows.
   - **Phone access:** turn it on and scan the QR code with your phone.
5. Click **Finish**: Egg Farm launches your executor and Roblox, and watches over both.

Egg Farm lives in the system tray (next to the clock): right-click its icon to open the dashboard, the settings, or to quit.

## Update

1. Right-click the Egg Farm icon in the tray, then **Quit**.
2. Download the new `EggFarm.exe` and replace the old one.
3. Run it again. Your settings, your key and your phone link are kept.

## FAQ

**My antivirus flags the file.**
Packaged Python programs are often flagged by mistake. Only download Egg Farm from this page.

**"This key is already used on another PC".**
Each key works on one PC. Ask the person who gave you the key to free it or to give you a new one.

**"Your key couldn't be checked".**
Egg Farm needs internet at least once a week to check its key. Connect the PC, then reopen the page.

**The dashboard stays empty.**
Make sure auto-execute is on in your executor, then reinstall the scripts from the settings and restart Roblox.

**How do I uninstall it?**
In the settings, turn off *Start with Windows*, then quit Egg Farm. Delete `EggFarm.exe`, the `%APPDATA%\EggFarm` folder, and the `eggfarm-*.luau` files in your executor's `autoexec` folder.
